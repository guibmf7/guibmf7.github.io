Rotation Money: privacy

Rotation Money is a personal budgeting app used by one person, its owner, for his own bank accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • Account balances and transactions are read through Enable Banking with the owner's consent, and only for accounts he has linked.
  • All data is stored only on the owner's phone. There is no server, no analytics and no sharing with third parties.
  • Access is read-only. The app cannot make payments or move money.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time in the app, in Revolut, or in the Enable Banking control panel.
